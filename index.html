What the bump actually is

Your thigh muscle pulls on your kneecap, the kneecap pulls on a tendon, and that tendon anchors into a small bump on the top of your shin. On a 12-year-old that anchor is not finished bone yet — it is a growth plate, still soft and still building.

So the tendon is pulling hard on a construction site. That tug is the ache. It is called Osgood-Schlatter, roughly 1 in 10 sporty teenagers get it, and it is not damage. It is an area being asked for more than it can currently give.

Stairs are the thing most likely to remind you it is there, and that is normal — going up and down loads exactly that spot. Keep using them.

Tell someone straight away if…

You feel a sudden pop in the knee while jumping or kicking, and afterwards you cannot straighten your leg or put weight on it. Stop, do not walk it off, say so immediately. It is rare, and it is a different thing from this.
